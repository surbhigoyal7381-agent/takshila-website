FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the batch size at Takshila Jagadhri?
We maintain small batch sizes of 25–30 students per batch to ensure personalised attention for each student. This is one of our key differentiators from large coaching centres.
Which classes/grades are eligible to join?
We offer programs for students from Class 8 onwards. Foundation programs start at Class 8–10, while JEE and NEET coaching is available for Class 11, 12, and Droppers.
Does Takshila offer online coaching as well?
Yes. We offer both offline (classroom) and online coaching options. Online students get access to live classes, recorded video lectures, digital study material, and online test series.
Are there scholarship opportunities available?
Yes, Takshila conducts scholarship examinations for meritorious students. Based on performance, students can receive fee waivers ranging from 10% to 100% of the course fee.
How does doubt-solving work?
We have dedicated daily doubt-clearing sessions with subject teachers. Students can also raise doubts via WhatsApp and our mobile app for quick resolution outside class hours.
What is the fee structure for JEE/NEET courses?
Fees vary based on the program and mode (online/offline). Please contact our Jagadhri centre at +91 89014 84127 or visit us for a personalised fee consultation and scholarship assessment.
